## About Me

I am an **AI and Software Engineer based in Dubai, UAE**, with an MSc in Artificial Intelligence and more than four years of experience building intelligent software and data-driven products.

My work focuses on transforming complex business processes into reliable, secure, and scalable systems using:

- Agentic AI and multi-agent orchestration
- Large language models and retrieval-augmented generation
- Machine learning and predictive analytics
- Document AI, OCR, and automated validation
- Real-time voice and conversational AI
- Backend platforms, APIs, and workflow automation

I enjoy taking AI products from early prototypes to production-ready systems while aligning technical decisions with measurable business outcomes.

`LangGraph` `Python` `FastAPI` `LLMs` `RAG` `Tool Calling`

`OCR` `Computer Vision` `Python` `.NET` `SQL Server`

`LiveKit` `ElevenLabs` `FastAPI` `LLMs`

`Python` `Pandas` `Scikit-learn` `TensorFlow` `SQL`
